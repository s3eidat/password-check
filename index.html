How the estimate works

The checker finds every pattern in your password: 1,282 common passwords, 2,381 English words (also reversed, capitalised or with l33t swaps like @→a), keyboard walks (qwerty, 1qaz), sequences (abc, 9876), repeats (aaa, abcabc), years and dates, then picks the cheapest way to cover the whole password and counts the guesses that takes. The word lists are small so they fit in the page, which means unusual words or names it doesn't know may score higher than they deserve. Treat the result as an upper bound.
